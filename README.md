# Meridian Mission Control

MERIDIAN GROUP USA



COMPLETE LOVABLE WEBSITE & GOVERNMENT CONTRACTING PLATFORM — MASTER BUILD PROMPT



PROJECT MISSION



Build a complete, modern, professional government contracting website and integrated business management platform for Meridian Group USA.



Website: https://meridiangroupusa.com



The platform must present Meridian Group USA as a credible government contracting business while providing the infrastructure to manage federal contracting opportunities, solicitations, compliance requirements, business development, proposals, and customer relationships.



Build a premium, enterprise-quality application that is easy to navigate, mobile-friendly, secure, scalable, and ready for future expansion.



Do not build a simple landing page. Build a complete company website with an integrated government contracting management system.



If an existing Lovable project is open, inspect and preserve its functional components and data before implementing the new design.



Do not overwrite existing customer information, authentication, or integrations.

PHASE 3 — FEDERAL SOLICITATION MANAGEMENT



Create a complete solicitation management workspace.



Supported Solicitation Types



- Request for Information (RFI).

- Sources Sought.

- Request for Quotation (RFQ).

- Request for Proposal (RFP).

- Invitation for Bid (IFB).

- Combined Synopsis/Solicitation.

- Amendments and modifications.



Solicitation Intake



Allow authorized users to:



- Upload solicitation documents.

- Upload PDF and Word files.

- Enter solicitation numbers.

- Enter agency and contracting office.

- Record NAICS and PSC codes.

- Record set-aside requirements.

- Enter submission deadlines.

- Attach amendments.

- Record contracting officer information.

- Add notes and supporting documents.



Provide a structured document viewer.



AI-Assisted Solicitation Analysis



Design an AI-assisted workflow to identify:



- Scope of work.

- Statement of Work.

- Performance Work Statement.

- Statement of Objectives.

- Technical requirements.

- Delivery requirements.

- Submission instructions.

- Evaluation factors.

- Pricing instructions.

- Mandatory attachments.

- Applicable contract clauses.

- Required certifications.

- Eligibility conditions.

- Deadlines and amendments.



Support Sections L and M when present, but also identify requirements appearing elsewhere.



Every extracted requirement must reference its original document and location.



Do not fabricate citations or missing requirements.



Label uncertain information for human review.

PHASE 4 — FAR & DFARS COMPLIANCE MANAGEMENT



Build a structured compliance management system that supports applicable federal acquisition requirements.



Include:



- Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR).

- Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS), when applicable.

- Agency-specific acquisition supplements.

- Solicitation-specific provisions and clauses.

- Contractual reporting requirements.

- Applicable small-business requirements.



Create a clause and requirements library with:



- Regulation identifier.

- Clause number.

- Clause title.

- Version or effective date.

- Applicability.

- Official source reference.

- Review status.



Link to authoritative government sources, including acquisition.gov and SAM.gov.



Do not represent the system as automatically guaranteeing legal or contractual compliance.



Compliance Matrix



Create a compliance matrix containing:



- Requirement ID.

- Requirement description.

- Source citation.

- Applicable regulation.

- Required action.

- Responsible user.

- Supporting evidence.

- Completion status.

- Reviewer approval.



Provide clear warnings for unresolved mandatory requirements.
